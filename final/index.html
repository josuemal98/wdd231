Welcome to Valley Pet Rescue

We are a dedicated non-profit shelter connecting homeless pets with loving families across Salt Lake County. Browse our adoptable animals, learn about our care procedures, or submit an adoption application today.

Our Mission

Valley Pet Rescue is dedicated to rescuing lost, abandoned, or surrendered animals. We provide medical rehabilitation, shelter, and foster care while connecting pet parents with their ideal companions under our motto: Adopt, Don't Shop.

How the Adoption Works

  1. Explore our dynamic directory under Adoptable Pets.
  2. Click on any pet to view detailed health records and biological history.
  3. Submit your official adoption application on our Join page.
