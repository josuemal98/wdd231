NP Membership Details

Designed for non-profit organizations looking for community support.

  • Free annual membership
  • Directory listing
  • Access to chamber community events

Bronze Membership Details

Great entry-level option for small businesses looking to grow.

  • Standard directory listing
  • Invitation to monthly networking meetings
  • Discounted event tickets

Silver Membership Details

Increased presence and promotional opportunities for growing businesses.

  • Enhanced directory listing with logo
  • Spotlight eligibility on homepage
  • Social media promotions twice a year

Gold Membership Details

Top-tier benefits for maximum business exposure and impact.

  • Featured position on homepage spotlights
  • Premium directory placement
  • Sponsorship priority for major events
  • Quarterly social media features
